For Ticket Holders

Know Before You Go

Everything you need for your night at Harbor Glow.

The short version

  • Where1332 South Harbor Blvd, San Pedro, CA 90731 (West Harbor at Port of Los Angeles)
  • HoursFri–Sun 5:00–10:30 PM · Mon–Thu 5:30–9:30 PM
    Christmas Eve & New Year’s Eve 5:00–10:30 PM
  • Arrive15 minutes before your entry window, plus time to park
  • BringYour tickets on your phone, warm layers and comfortable shoes
1

Getting here and parking

  • Premium Event Parking is right at the entrance. Your space is guaranteed if you bought a pass with your tickets. On site, it’s $15 plus fees and taxes, while spaces last.
  • General Parking is West Harbor’s public lot, a longer walk. First come, first served, paid on site.
  • Accessible spaces are in both lots, closest in Premium Event Parking. Display your placard and pay the lot’s standard rate.
  • Rideshare: drop off at the address above.
2

At the gate

  • Bag check first, then ticket scan. Have your tickets open on your phone.
  • We admit guests up to 30 minutes after your entry window opens. After that, we’ll try to fit you in later, but can’t guarantee it.
  • Bags up to about 16 × 16 × 8 inches; no coolers or large luggage.
  • No re-entry, so bring everything you need from the car.
3

Dress for the harbor

It gets windy and cold by the water at night, often colder than at home. Bring a warm jacket, layers, and hats and gloves for little ones.

4

Once you’re in

  • Take your time. Most guests spend 30 to 40 minutes. Big enough to explore, small enough for little legs.
  • Dino Ride: an optional, self-driven dinosaur ride. Buy tokens at the Dino Token Booth or add them to your order. Riders must be 3+. A child can ride with an adult if the total weight is under 200 lbs. Tokens are non-refundable and valid any night this season.
  • Food and drinks: vendors vary by night, and the lineup can change. No outside food or drinks, except baby food and medical or dietary items.
  • Need help? Find the Guest Services tent or any staff member.
  • Photos: personal photos and video are welcome. Tripods, drones and paid photo sessions need our written approval in advance.
5

Weather and Rain Checks

  • Harbor Glow is outdoors. We may cancel or close a night for rain, high winds or other safety reasons.
  • We usually decide by about 2:00 PM and notify ticket holders by email and text.
  • If we cancel your night, your tickets automatically become Rain Checks, good for any remaining night through January 3, 2027. Premium Event Parking passes move with them.
  • If we close after you arrive, it depends on when your ticket was scanned. See Weather & Changes in our FAQ.
6

Plans changed?

Change your date or time yourself, up to 3 times per ticket and at least 48 hours ahead. You can also send tickets to someone else.

7

Good to know

  • Strollers and wheelchairs are welcome. Some areas are artificial turf, which takes a little more effort to push through.
  • Guests under 18 must be with an adult.
  • No pets; service animals are welcome.
  • No smoking, vaping or alcohol. Full list in our FAQ.

Questions?

Email info@harborglownights.com with your order number. See you at the harbor.

Hours, vendors and operating details may change.
