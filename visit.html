Harbor Glow Premium Parking

Our dedicated lot, right at the Harbor Glow entrance. Reserve it with your tickets and drive straight in.

Add a Premium Parking pass when you buy tickets; the price is shown before checkout. Spaces are limited. Your pass is valid for the date shown, and moves with your ticket if you change dates or receive a Rain Check.

General parking: West Harbor's public lot, a longer walk from the entrance. It's first come, first served and paid on site at West Harbor's rates. Harbor Glow does not sell passes for it.
