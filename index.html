The Experience

One Night. Endless Light.

A self-guided journey through three glowing worlds built along the water's edge — from crystal blooms to a reef of bioluminescent light.

The Crystal Ice Garden

Towering illuminated ice-blooms rise overhead in a hand-built garden of light. Every petal glows, every path is a new photo waiting to happen.

Winter Crystal Grove

Wander a forest of towering crystal trees under a canopy of stars, anchored by a glowing ice-heart made for the perfect holiday portrait.

The Enchanted Reef

Drift beneath a sky of glowing jellyfish and coral in an otherworldly underwater walk — a reef of light suspended over the harbor promenade.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Harbor Glow is located at 1332 South Harbor Blvd, San Pedro, CA. On-site and nearby waterfront parking is available — we recommend arriving 30 minutes before your entry time. Parking details and pre-pay options will be posted once tickets go on sale.

Harbor Glow is a mostly outdoor, rain-or-shine experience — the lights look magical in a light mist. If severe weather forces a closure, your tickets will be honored on another available night or refunded per our weather policy, which will be posted once tickets go on sale.

Children 2 and under are free and do not need a ticket. Kids 3 and up will require a child ticket at a reduced price, with family 4-packs offering the best value — full pricing goes live with tickets.

Most guests move through the Ice Garden, the Crystal Grove, and the Enchanted Reef in about 40 minutes to an hour. There's no rush — once you're in, you're welcome to linger until closing.

Yes. The main light path is flat and fully accessible for strollers and wheelchairs. Accessible parking and restrooms are available on site.

Only trained service animals are permitted at Harbor Glow. We're unable to accommodate other pets, including emotional support animals, given the density of the light installations and crowd flow along the path.
