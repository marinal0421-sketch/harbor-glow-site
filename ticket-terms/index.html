2. Tickets, pricing and fees

A ticket is a revocable license for one person to enter the Event at the date and entry time shown. It is not property and has no cash value.

  • All-in pricing. Displayed ticket prices include all mandatory fees. Optional add-ons, such as parking, are priced separately and shown before checkout.
  • Prices vary. Prices vary by date and demand, may be higher on the day of the visit and at the box office, and may change at any time. Glow Works does not adjust prices or refund differences if a price drops after purchase.
  • Ticket types. Children under 3 enter free but need a free ticket for capacity. Child tickets are for ages 3–12. Senior tickets are for guests 65 and older, and military tickets are for current or former service members. Glow Works may ask for proof of age or eligibility at entry. A guest who cannot show eligibility when asked must pay the difference to the applicable ticket price.
  • Discounts and promo codes. Only one discount or promo code applies per ticket. Codes cannot be combined, have no cash value and cannot be applied to past purchases.
  • Order limit. Up to 10 tickets per order. For groups of more than 10, contact info@harborglownights.com.
  • Check your order. Holders should check the date, entry time and ticket types before paying. Changes after purchase follow Section 4.
  • Lost tickets. Glow Works can resend tickets to the purchaser's email address.
  • Box office. On-site purchases are subject to these same Terms.

3. All sales final

All sales are final. Glow Works does not give full or partial refunds for any reason, except as required by law. This includes weather, traffic, illness, late arrival, partial closure of displays or attractions, and removal for conduct.

  • If the Holder's plans change, the remedy is a date change under Section 4.
  • If Glow Works cancels or closes a night, the remedy is a Rain Check under Section 5, not a refund.
  • Fees on refunds. If Glow Works makes any refund, whether as an exception or because the law requires it, the fees included in the ticket price are not refunded, unless the law requires otherwise.

4. Date changes

Date changes are free. Holders can change their own date or entry time, as many times as they like, through the self-serve ticket portal linked in their order confirmation email and on harborglownights.com. Changes are subject to availability.

  • Price difference. Moving to a higher-priced night means paying the difference. Moving to a lower-priced night does not refund the difference.
  • Cut-off. Changes close 48 hours before the original entry time. A missed entry time cannot be changed or refunded.
  • Season only. Tickets are valid for the 2026–27 season only and do not carry over to a later season.

5. Weather, closures and Rain Checks

Harbor Glow is a rain-or-shine outdoor event. Rain, cold, and closure of individual displays, attractions or areas do not qualify for a refund or a Rain Check.

Safety closures. Glow Works may delay, suspend, close early or cancel all or part of any night if Glow Works judges that weather or other conditions make operation unsafe. Examples include high winds, lightning, heavy rain or flooding, official weather warnings, and power or equipment failure.

Notice. Glow Works aims to decide on a full-night cancellation by about 2:00 p.m. Pacific time that day, but may cancel, delay or close later if conditions change suddenly. Glow Works will notify purchasers by email and/or text message and post updates on harborglownights.com and social media. Purchasers must keep their contact details current.

When a ticket becomes a Rain Check. A ticket automatically becomes a Rain Check, with no action needed, if either of these happens:

  • Glow Works cancels the night before the ticket's entry time; or
  • Glow Works closes the Event within 60 minutes after the start of the ticket's entry time.

If Glow Works closes the Event more than 60 minutes after the start of the entry time, the ticket counts as used and no Rain Check is issued.

Using a Rain Check. Reserve any remaining operating night this season through the ticket portal, subject to availability.

  • Nights at the same or a lower price are free.
  • For a higher-priced night, pay the difference.
  • The 48-hour change cut-off does not apply to reserving a Rain Check.
  • Once reserved, the Rain Check becomes a regular date ticket under Section 4.
  • If the reserved night is also cancelled, a new Rain Check is issued.

Rain Checks have no cash value, cannot be refunded and expire at the close of the season on January 3, 2027.

Glow Works is not responsible for travel, parking, lodging, childcare or other costs caused by a delay, closure or cancellation (see Section 12).

6. Entry times, admission and length of stay

Each ticket is for a 30-minute entry window that starts at the time shown on the ticket.

  • Arriving early. Arrive no more than 15 minutes before the entry window starts.
  • Grace period. Holders are admitted up to 30 minutes after the start of their entry window.
  • Late arrivals. After the grace period, Glow Works may admit Holders in a later entry window if capacity allows. This is not guaranteed. Missed entry times are not refundable and cannot be changed after the fact.
  • Length of stay. There is no time limit. Holders may stay until that night's closing time. Most visits take about 40–60 minutes.
  • Crowd management. Glow Works may hold entry, meter guest flow, or close areas temporarily.
  • No re-entry. Leaving the Event ends the visit.
  • Schedule. The Event operates nightly from November 20, 2026 to January 3, 2027. Hours are 5:30–9:30 p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and 5:00–10:30 p.m. on Fridays, Saturdays and holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day). Glow Works may change operating hours.

  • Ticket validity. A ticket admits only the guests and uses it includes. Glow Works is not responsible for duplicated, copied, counterfeit, lost or stolen tickets.
  • Bots. Using automated software to buy tickets is prohibited.
  • Fraud. Glow Works may cancel orders placed fraudulently, such as with stolen payment details, and tickets that are counterfeit.
  • Passing on the Terms. Anyone who transfers a ticket should tell the recipient that these Terms and the Waiver apply. The recipient accepts them by using the ticket.

8. Children, accessibility and service animals

  • Supervision. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or older at all times. The accompanying adult accepts the Waiver on the minor's behalf and is responsible for them.
  • Strollers and wagons. Strollers and wagons are permitted.
  • Accessibility. Wheelchairs and mobility devices are welcome. Some surfaces are uneven or may be wet. Guests needing accommodations should contact info@harborglownights.com at least 48 hours before their visit.
  • Light sensitivity. The Event includes flashing lights, fog and darkness. See the warning in the Waiver.
  • Service animals. Service animals as defined by the Americans with Disabilities Act are welcome and must stay under the handler's control. Pets and emotional support, comfort or therapy animals are not permitted.

9. Conduct, prohibited items and security

Holders must follow staff instructions and posted rules. Posted rules form part of these Terms.

Prohibited items:

  • Pets (service animals excepted, see Section 8)
  • Alcohol of any kind, illegal drugs and cannabis
  • Weapons, fireworks, laser pointers and hazardous materials
  • Glass containers
  • Drones
  • Tripods, monopods and light stands (see Section 10)
  • Bicycles, scooters, skateboards, hoverboards and skates
  • Amplified speakers
  • Bags larger than 16 x 16 x 8 inches (a standard backpack). Diaper bags and medical bags are allowed. No coolers, suitcases or large luggage.
  • Outside food, except baby food and items needed for medical or dietary reasons

Rules:

  • No smoking or vaping anywhere at the Event, including cannabis.
  • Stay on marked paths. Do not climb on, touch, move or damage displays.
  • No selling, soliciting or handing out materials without Glow Works' written consent.
  • Bags may be searched at entry. Glow Works may refuse entry to anyone who declines.

Removal. Glow Works may refuse entry to, or remove, anyone who appears intoxicated, behaves in a disorderly, unsafe or harassing way, damages property, or ignores staff instructions. No refund is given. Holders are responsible for any damage they cause.

Personal property. Glow Works is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged property. For lost and found, contact info@harborglownights.com.

  • Tripods, monopods and light stands are not permitted during public hours.
  • Commercial photography or video needs Glow Works' written permission in advance. This includes hired photographers, paid sessions (maternity, family, engagement), content productions and drones. Requests go to info@harborglownights.com.
  • Proposals are welcome. Props, décor or set-ups need Glow Works' approval in advance.

Media consent. Consent to being photographed and recorded at the Event, and to the use of those images, is covered in the Waiver (Section 4).

  • Parking lots are operated under West Harbor's rules. Vehicles are parked at the owner's risk.
  • Unauthorized or improperly parked vehicles may be towed at the owner's expense.
