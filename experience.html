The Experience

Winter Dreamscape to World Beneath the Waves.

A self-guided walk through two illuminated worlds along the water's edge. The path opens at a glowing ice-crystal gate, moves through a snow castle and crystal forest, then turns into a bioluminescent ocean of jellyfish, coral, and a giant whale — seventeen scenes in all, across roughly 80,000–90,000 square feet of light.

Concept imagery — design concept, final execution may vary.

Chapter One

Winter Dreamscape

An Immersive Journey Through Crystal and Light

Eleven scenes make up Winter Dreamscape, the larger of the two worlds — crystal gateways, glowing forests, reflective surfaces and large photo moments, from the ice gate at the entrance to the Pegasus Carriage centerpiece. Five signature scenes are pictured below; more photos will follow as the rest of the installations are completed.

Ice & Snow Gate

The towering crystal gateway that opens the walk into Winter Dreamscape.

Christmas Wonderland

Santa, a gingerbread friend, and a glowing snowman family — the largest single piece in the whole walk.

Christmas Tree

A grove of towering crystal trees built around a glowing ice heart.

Crystal Ice Garden

Towering illuminated ice-blooms rise overhead in a hand-built garden of light.

Pegasus Carriage

A winged carriage centerpiece — one of the walk's largest photo moments.

Chapter Two

World Beneath the Waves

A Bioluminescent Ocean Journey

Six scenes make up World Beneath the Waves — illuminated whales, jellyfish, coral and interactive details that connect naturally to the waterfront, from the Jellyfish Gate at the turn in the path to the giant winged whale at Deep Sea Encounter. Coral Archway and Fairytale Ocean are still in production, with photos to come.

Jellyfish Gate

Jellyfish suspended in light mark the turn from ice into ocean.

Ocean Exploration

The harbor's largest reef comes to life — a darting school of tropical fish, octopus, and crab.

Coral Secret Realm

A sea turtle, a dolphin, and a whale-skeleton fossil hidden in the coral.

Deep Sea Encounter

A mermaid on a shell beside a giant winged-whale centerpiece.
