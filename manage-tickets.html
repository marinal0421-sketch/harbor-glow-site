Ticket Help

Manage Your Tickets

Change your entry time or date, upgrade your tickets, or send them to someone else. You do it all yourself in your Harbor Glow ticket account.

Open My Ticket Account

Already registered? Choose “Login instead” on that page.

Before you change

  • Up to 3 changes per ticketA new date or a new entry time each counts as one change.
  • At least 48 hours aheadChanges close 48 hours before your original entry time.
  • Free, plus any differenceHigher-priced night: pay the difference. Lower-priced night: no refund of the difference.
  • This season onlySubject to availability. Missed entry times can't be changed afterward.

Rain Checks from a cancelled night work differently. See Weather & Changes in our FAQ. Full rules are in Section 4 of our Ticketing Terms.

1

Create your account

Do this once. If you already have an account, sign in instead.

  1. Go to tickets.harborglownights.com/my-account/register.
  2. Register with the same email address you used to buy your tickets. Your orders appear automatically.
  3. Enter your name, create a password, add your phone number and select Register.
2

Find your order

Every change starts here.

  1. Under Your orders, find the order and open the gear menu.
  2. Select Change Order to change a time, date or ticket type.
  3. To give tickets to someone else, select Send Ticket(s) instead (see step 6).
3

Change your entry time

Same night, different arrival time.

  1. On the Change Order screen, choose Change information.
  2. Select Change next to a ticket.
  3. Pick your new time slot and select Update Information.
  4. Repeat for each ticket. Tickets are updated one at a time.
4

Change your date

Move your tickets to another night this season.

  1. On the Change Order screen, choose Switch to a different event.
  2. Select the new date and the ticket you want to move.
  3. Choose a time slot and select Update Information.
  4. Repeat for each ticket. If the new night costs more, pay the difference to finish.
5

Upgrade your tickets

Switch to a different ticket type, for example a Family Pack.

  1. On the Change Order screen, choose Upgrade tickets.
  2. Select the ticket to upgrade and choose the new ticket type. Repeat for any other tickets.
  3. Review the amount due and complete payment.

Prices in these screenshots are examples. Your total may differ.

6

Send tickets to someone else

Give a ticket to a friend or family member.

  1. Under Your orders, open the gear menu and select Send Ticket(s).
  2. Tick the box next to each ticket you want to send.
  3. Enter the recipient's first name, last name and email, then select Send Tickets.

Please let the recipient know our Ticketing Terms apply to them too.

Still need help?

Email info@harborglownights.com with your order number and we'll sort it out.
